# HackDevengers 1.0 Final Submission

## Project Name
CivicFix AI

## One-line Pitch
CivicFix AI transforms unstructured community complaints into prioritized, actionable civic reports using AI.

## Problem
Everyday issues such as potholes, broken streetlights, garbage accumulation and drainage problems are often reported as unstructured text, making it harder to understand urgency and route issues efficiently.

## Solution
CivicFix AI lets a user describe a problem naturally. Gemini structures the report into category, urgency, summary, recommended action, responsible department and tags. A deterministic fallback keeps the prototype functional when AI is unavailable.

## Technology
React, Vite, Python, FastAPI, Pydantic, Google Gemini API, REST API, CSS.

## Impact
The prototype makes civic reporting simpler for users and creates structured information that can help prioritize and route community issues.

## Future Scope
Image-based issue detection, maps/GPS, duplicate detection, multilingual reporting, report history, department dashboards and recurring-issue analytics.

## Safety
AI-generated classifications are recommendations only and should be reviewed by responsible authorities.
